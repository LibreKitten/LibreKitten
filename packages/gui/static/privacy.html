LibreKitten Privacy Policy

Updated Feburary 14, 2026

The LibreKitten project respects your privacy. Every website says this, but we mean it: We aren't interested in your personal information. In the interest of full transparency, this document lists every place where your data might be collected or shared:

Loading projects

When you load a project from another website, you are subject to the privacy practices of that website. For example, when loading projects from Scratch, you are subject to the Scratch privacy policy. When loading projects from Scratch specifically, the project ID will also be shared with TurboWarp as part of Scratch's API does not allow direct access; TurboWarp is covered by their privacy policy.

Cloud variables

When connecting to our cloud variable servers, the project ID and username may be logged for 14 days. Data in cloud variables is public and sent to anyone else connected at the same time. Custom cloud variable servers are outside of our control.

Extensions

Some extensions must communicate with external APIs to function. For example, the translate and text-to-speech extensions rely on the Scratch API, which is covered by the Scratch privacy policy. To improve performance, some extensions access TurboWarp's APIs instead, which is covered by the TurboWarp privacy policy.

Most extensions on the TurboWarp extension gallery will ask for permission when the project attempts to use the extension to access an untrusted website, however we cannot guarantee this is 100% reliable.

When loading a custom extension from a place other than the TurboWarp gallery such as a URL or file on your computer, the editor will ask for permission to load the extension. If you approve this dialog, the extension can bypass the permission dialogs that official extensions show.

Settings

Our website will store settings such as addon settings in your browser.

Statistics

We use Counter for our statistics; Counter is covered by their privacy policy. Counter respects your privacy and does not save cookies nor log your IP address.

Contact

Any concerns related to privacy or any other matter should be sent via the feedback button on our home page.
